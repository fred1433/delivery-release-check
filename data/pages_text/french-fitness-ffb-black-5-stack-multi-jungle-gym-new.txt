Warranty
10 Years Parts, 1 Year Labor
for USA & Canada for all French Fitness equipment excluding free weights, flooring, and accessories.
1-Year Limited Warranty
for gym accessories, free weights, and flooring.
What Does It Include:
Covers any mechanical or electrical issues which prevent the machine to function in like-new condition
Covers 100% labor costs and unlimited on-site technician diagnostic and repair services
Includes 100% coverage for parts replacement, including structural and moving components, such as bearings, pulleys, pins, cables, motors, batteries, and any other parts necessary to maintain the machine in like-new working condition
Covers wearable parts such as heart rate monitors, pedals, treadmill decks, running belts, and track wheels which cause the machine to malfunction
Covers software and electronic issues, program malfunctions, and console resets
Warranty terms may vary depending on the usage, whether the equipment will be used in your home or in a commercial setting.
Commercial Grade Product Warranty
Intended for gyms, health clubs, and fitness centers, where a high volume of users will be utilizing the equipment continuously throughout the day.
Light Commercial Grade Product Warranty
Intended for settings like schools, police stations, hospitals, rehab centers, and hotels, where usage is expected to be around 2-4 hours per day.
Warranty is void when the equipment is used in high-traffic commercial settings.
Home Use Product Warranty
Tailored for personal use within home settings. With a usage recommendation of under one hour per day, these products are designed for individual or small-group workouts.
Warranty is void when the product is used in light commercial and commercial settings.
USA & Canada Warranty Terms
For customers within the USA and Canada, the warranty covers ALL parts for a period 10 years from the date of purchase.
The warranty also covers labor for a period 1 year from the date of purchase.
Types of equipment covered by the 10 years parts warranty and 1 year labor warranty are cardio equipment, gym systems, racks and cages, plate loaded equipment, benches, selectorized equipment, and storage racks.
Gym accessories, free weights, and flooring come with a 1 year limited parts warranty. If an item becomes non-functional due to breakage, we will replace it free of charge. Please note that cosmetic defects (such as chunks of rubber missing), gashes, and other wear and tear are not covered unless reported within 7 days of receipt of the items.
Cosmetic defects are covered for the first 7 days upon receipt of the items.
Labor warranty is extendable for $100/year on strength equipment and $150/year on cardio equipment.
How to Redeem Your French Fitness Warranty
Step 1:
Contact us to initiate the warranty claim process. Send us relevant photos or videos of the damage. We will then evaluate the best solution to address your concerns.
Step 2:
Our support team will confirm that we have received your queries within 24 hours, and will respond with the course of action shortly afterwards.
Step 3:
We will assess the necessary repairs and provide the required parts and detailed instructions. If the machine is no longer covered by the labor warranty, we will guide you through the installation or repair process. Alternatively, upon request, we can recommend qualified technicians in your area to handle the labor.
Step 4:
If your machine is covered by labor warranty, a technician will be dispatched within 1-2 weeks of receiving a service request.
Northern California: Warranty service will be performed by our in-house technicians.
Outside Northern California: Warranty service will be performed by a trusted member of our nationwide network of technicians.
Note 1:
The warranty will be void if the customer fails to show up for a scheduled service call, unless they pay the technician directly. A trip charge will apply once the technician is dispatched. If the customer fails to show for a scheduled service call and does not communicate with the technician, they will be responsible for the trip charge.
Note 2:
If we send parts under the warranty, we will cover all shipping charges for warrantied parts. The customer also agrees to return any unused, defective, or old parts using the provided return shipping label. Failure to return parts replaced will void the warranty.

ASSEMBLY & ROOM OF CHOICE INSTALLATION NEEDED?
No Thanks, Curbside Delivery Only
Your machine(s) are placed on the closest curb to your location accessible by a 24 foot box truck. If you don't have a curb it will be the end of the driveway / part of your property closest to the street. It is a 1 person delivery, and it will arrive in a crate. The truck will back up, use a liftgate, and drop the pallet off. The customer will be responsible for placing the machine where they want it, as well as removing / disposing of packaging. Some products will arrive disassembled, and some fully assembled. See the product page for more info, or contact us if you need any clarification on whether or not this method will apply to your situation. Estimated delivery timing is confirmed for the specific order after product readiness, destination, access, and provider availability are reviewed. Timing is not guaranteed until the applicable service is booked.
No Thanks, FedEx or Curbside Delivery
This means it will likely ship Fedex if ordered alone, or with another similar sized item, and drop off where most Fedex boxes drop off to. However if this is a a part of a bigger order, like a large item and this item are part of the same order, or many smaller items, and the size and weight become too costly for Fedex, it will ship via freight and deliver curbside.
'Garage Delivery', No Unpacking, Left in Crate
The LTL Freight Carrier we hire would come equipped with a liftgate, and a pallet jack, and the driver would take it as far as it could go on a pallet jack. This service only includes going across gravel / concrete, so, if there are barriers such as stairs, softer dirt, grass, a ledge, or a width that is less wide than the crate, then this service would not suffice. The carrier will not do any unpacking or debris removal with this service. Garage placement is available only when the selected provider and service explicitly include it and site access permits it. Curbside service does not include movement into a garage.
Alternative Installation: 2 Step Process
With this option, the Step 1 plan uses professional delivery and, when needed, movement provider(s) to place the packaged equipment in an approved dry, secure, accessible, ground-level temporary storage area at your location. The equipment typically remains packaged there for approximately 1–7 days, unless a different estimated interval is confirmed. A professional installer then comes on a later date to move the equipment from storage to the final room, complete the agreed assembly/installation and function check, and remove the packaging and debris.
You are not responsible for unloading the delivery truck, carrying or pallet-jacking the freight, moving the equipment, or assembling it.
Any preliminary response about this option authorizes us to investigate and quote it only; it is not final authorization. Before anything is booked, released, or shipped under this option, we will provide the exact delivery provider, service level, handoff point, temporary storage point, movement owner, installer, crew, included and excluded scope, all-inclusive price, and estimated timing for your final written authorization. The Alternative Installation will proceed only after we receive your final written YES.
If this option is not suitable, the Standard Installation - Shipping to Terminal or Installer (1 Step Process) remains available. Please contact us with questions about which method may fit your location.

GRADE: HOME, LIGHT COMMERCIAL, OR COMMERCIAL?
When setting up a fitness space, it's important to consider the type of gym products that best suit your needs. There are three main categories to choose from: commercial, light commercial, and home-grade gym products. Each category caters to different usage scenarios and comes with its own set of features and durability considerations.
Commercial Grade Fitness Products:
Engineered for the demands of professional fitness facilities. Built to withstand 8-12 hours of daily use, they are constructed with the utmost durability and robustness in mind. These products are intended for gyms, health clubs, and fitness centers, where a high volume of users will be utilizing the equipment continuously throughout the day.
Light Commercial Grade Fitness Products:
Occupy a middle ground between full commercial and home-grade equipment. Intended for settings like schools, police stations, hospitals, rehab centers, and hotels, where usage is expected to be around 2-4 hours per day, these products are more resilient than home-grade options but not as heavy-duty as their commercial counterparts.
Home Grade Fitness Products:
Tailored for personal use within home settings. With a usage recommendation of under one hour per day, these products are designed for individual or small-group workouts. They are designed to be more compact, affordable, and suitable for smaller spaces. While they might not offer the same level of durability as commercial or light commercial equipment, they are built to withstand moderate use by individuals or small groups.
Ultimately, the choice between these categories depends on your specific fitness goals, the intended usage of the equipment, and your budget. Whether you're looking to equip a bustling gym, a smaller-scale fitness space, or your personal workout haven at home, understanding these categories will help you make an informed decision about the gym products that best align with your needs.

Why Add a 5 Stack Multi Jungle Gym to Your Gym Floor
A
5 stack multi jungle gym
allows multiple users to train at the same time, making it ideal for busy commercial gyms and group training environments. By combining several cable stations into one integrated system, it maximizes exercise variety while improving floor space efficiency.
The French Fitness FFB Black 5 Stack Multi Jungle Gym supports structured group workouts, circuit training, and individual strength sessions, helping facilities reduce wait times and keep training areas organized and productive.
Buying Guide: Choosing a 5 Stack Multi Jungle Gym
When selecting a
5 stack multi jungle gym
, focus on user capacity, station layout, and long-term durability. A multi jungle gym should support multiple users training simultaneously while offering a mix of fixed stations and adjustable pulleys to accommodate different exercises and body sizes.
Pay close attention to frame construction, pulley quality, and cable specifications, as these components affect smoothness and reliability in high-traffic gyms. Facilities should also consider available floor space, ceiling height, and workout flow. A well-designed 5 stack system helps reduce wait times, supports group training, and delivers efficient strength training within a single, space-optimized footprint.

Its heavy-duty steel construction, selectorized weight stacks, and modular layout make it a reliable solution for gyms, training studios, and athletic facilities that require dependable performance under daily use. Whether supporting group workouts, circuit training, or individual strength sessions, the 5 Stack Multi Jungle Gym provides a structured, space-efficient platform for long-term training needs.
Frequently Asked Questions
1. How many users can train on the 5 Stack Multi Jungle Gym at the same time?
The system is designed to support up to five users training simultaneously, each using a different station without interference.
2. What stations are included with the standard configuration?
The standard setup includes one lat pulldown station, one low row station, one triceps extension station, two adjustable pulley stations, and one integrated pull-up station.
3. Is this machine suitable for commercial gym use?
Yes. The 5 Stack Multi Jungle Gym is built with commercial-grade steel construction, selectorized weight stacks, and sealed-bearing pulleys to support consistent daily use in shared training environments.
4. Are the pulley heights adjustable for different users?
Yes. The adjustable pulley stations feature multiple vertical height settings, allowing users of different sizes to maintain proper positioning during exercises.
5. Can this jungle gym be used for group or circuit training?
Yes. The multi-station layout and independent weight stacks make it well-suited for group training sessions, circuit workouts, and athletic conditioning programs.
