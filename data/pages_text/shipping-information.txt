Free local shipping and pickup
Free Local Delivery on Eligible $1,000+ Orders
Fitness Superstore offers free local delivery on eligible orders of $1,000 or more to approved delivery ZIP codes within approximately 100 miles of 94510. Eligibility is determined at checkout using the delivery ZIP and qualifying cart subtotal. Addresses outside the approved ZIP list, and orders below the qualifying subtotal, use the available paid delivery options shown at checkout.
Free warehouse pickup is also available; select pickup at checkout and wait for readiness and pickup confirmation. In-store pickup address: 537 Stone Rd STE F, Benicia, CA 94510.
Choose your delivery service
Curbside freight: delivery to the closest accessible curbside area. The customer moves the equipment from curbside, unpacks it and disposes of the packaging. Equipment may arrive assembled or disassembled as stated on the product page.
Garage delivery, no unpacking: equipment is left in its crate in the garage. The customer handles movement beyond the garage, unpacking and packaging disposal. Confirm driveway and pallet-jack access before booking.
Parcel: smaller eligible items travel by parcel carrier or our local vehicle. Parcel delivery does not include assembly.
Room of choice / assembly: select the service offered for the item. The purchased scope determines placement, unpacking, assembly and packaging removal. Provide room location, doorway/path measurements, stairs and access photos before booking. Do not assume these services are included with curbside, parcel or free shipping. For a two-step delivery-and-installation arrangement, transportation and the assembler's appointment are confirmed separately; the transit estimate is not an installation-completion date.
Canada and international orders
Availability, transportation, duties, taxes, customs clearance and onward delivery depend on the destination and written quote. CIF covers transportation to the specified destination port, with destination charges and onward arrangements handled by the buyer as quoted. DDP covers delivery to the named destination under the quoted inclusive scope. EXW places pickup and transportation arrangements with the buyer. Confirm the quoted responsibilities before ordering.
Receiving, damage and delivery changes
Inspect the shipment and packaging at delivery. For visible damage, note "shipping damage" on the delivery receipt and contact us. You may accept or reject a damaged shipment. For minor or moderate damage, we recommend accepting delivery; we will send replacement parts and an installer to fix the damaged part. You may instead reject delivery and contact us to discuss the next steps.
Provide accurate contact and delivery/access information. Additional charges apply for cancellation after the item is loaded, being late for the agreed delivery appointment, storage when the customer does not respond to the shipper within five business days of its contact attempt, or a failed room-of-choice delivery caused by missing or inaccurate access information. Contact us when delivery is late, incomplete or needs to change.
Questions? Call 925-215-2927. Product preparation, shipment arrival and any installation appointment must be confirmed separately.
