Shipping policy
For full details on our shipping options, delivery process, and international shipping, please visit our dedicated page i.e Shipping Information
Delivery Options
Curbside Delivery
Delivered to the nearest accessible curb via a 24-foot box truck. Estimated transit: 2–14 business days (lower 48 states). One delivery person; customer is responsible for moving and unpacking the equipment.
Garage Delivery (No Unpacking)
Equipment is brought to your garage using a liftgate and pallet jack. Available on gravel and concrete surfaces only. Same transit timeframe as curbside.
Small Parcel Delivery
For items under 150 lbs. Shipped via USPS, FedEx, or UPS. Customers within 120 miles of our Benicia, CA warehouse receive delivery via our own trucks. Estimated transit: 1–6 business days.
Room of Choice Delivery
An optional upgrade that may include inside delivery to a specific room, packaging removal, and assembly. Available options:
Garage Installation
0–3 Steps — Room of Choice, Fully Assembled
4+ Steps — Room of Choice, Fully Assembled
Assembly – Any Room (2-step process: delivery first, then separate assembly appointment)
Estimated transit: 5–17 business days.
Estimated Delivery Times
Total delivery time = Processing Time + Transit Time. A processing and transit time calculator is available on each product page.
Brand New Equipment:
Ships within 2–7 business days
Remanufactured Equipment:
2–5 weeks
Room of Choice / Freight:
5–17 business days (contiguous U.S.)
International:
Varies by freight method
Shipping Costs
Shipping costs vary by location and delivery method. To calculate your shipping fee, add items to your cart and enter your ZIP code at checkout.
International Shipping
We ship worldwide. Items under 150 lbs ship via FedEx International Ground. Items 150 lbs or more ship via ocean freight. VAT/GST and customs fees may apply depending on your destination.
Available shipping terms:
EXW (Ex Works)
– Customer is responsible for all pickup, transport, and customs fees
CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)
– We cover shipping to the largest destination port; customer handles customs and onward delivery
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)
– All customs, duties, and transport costs are included in the price
Northern California & In-Store Pickup
Customers within 120–200 miles of our Benicia, CA warehouse receive delivery via our own trucks, with free shipping and 50% off assembly. In-store pickup is also available at no extra charge.
Pickup Address:

Damaged Deliveries
Minor Damage:
Accept the delivery, note "shipping damage" on the delivery receipt, and contact us. We will arrange replacement parts and an installer.
Major Damage:
Photograph all packaging and equipment, write "major shipping damage" on the delivery receipt, reject the delivery, and contact us for next steps.
For damage or delivery issues, please email:
sales.cs@fitnesssuperstore.com
